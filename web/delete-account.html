Account & Data Deletion Request

At TigerBay International, we are committed to upholding your digital privacy and ensuring you have complete control over your personal data under GDPR, UK Data Protection laws, and Google Play Data Safety policies.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Method 1: Direct In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)
  1. Open the TigerBay app on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the navigation bar.
  3. Tap Edit Profile.
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the screen and select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your request. Your profile, credentials, and digital pass will be permanently removed immediately.
Method 2: Email Concierge Request

If you no longer have access to the app, you may email our data protection team directly at:
privacy@tigerbayshisha.co.uk or info@tigerbayshisha.co.uk
Please include your registered phone number or email address with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Requests are verified and processed within 14 business days.

Types of Data That Are Deleted

  • Personal Identity Data: Full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and avatar photos.
  • Authentication Data: Login credentials, OAuth associations, and session tokens.
  • Device Identifiers: 1:1 hardware device binding fingerprints used for anti-fraud pass generation.
  • Loyalty Data: Accumulated Strips balance, reward redemption history, and dynamic QR pass records.

Data Retention Policy (Data Kept & Period)

To comply with legal, financial, and tax obligations (such as UK HMRC statutory accounting requirements), anonymized payment transaction references, booking deposit invoices, and fraud prevention audit logs are retained for up to 6 years following the date of the transaction. This financial record is strictly severed from your personal profile and identity.
